Delta Maps

A map for getting around the Paraná Delta (Argentina) by motorboat, sailboat or kayak: rivers, streams and docks, directions by water and alerts before every turn.

  • Rivers, streams, docks, marinas, fuel and places to eat, with search.
  • Directions by water from where you are, for your type of boat.
  • Navigation with spoken turn alerts, even with the screen off.
  • Live speed (km/h or knots) and heading.
  • River level at a glance and passenger launch schedules.
  • On iPhone, an Apple Watch app that vibrates before every turn.

Before you go: Delta Maps is an aid to help you find your way. It does not replace nautical charts, waterway signs or Argentine Coast Guard (Prefectura Naval) instructions. It does not know depths, bridges or obstacles: you decide where to go.

The first 14 days are free. After that, Delta Maps Pro: US$2.99 per month or US$24.99 per year. It renews automatically until you cancel; cancel before the trial ends and you won't be charged. On iPhone it includes Marea Delta Pro.
