Marea Delta

The river level in your area of the Paraná Delta (Argentina), with an hourly forecast and alerts before it rises or falls. The app is in Spanish.

  • Live levels from INA and Coast Guard gauges, from Rosario to Buenos Aires.
  • Hourly forecast with its error margin.
  • The whole Delta on a map and zone comparison.
  • Level alerts, even with the app closed.

The first 7 days are free and complete, no card needed. After that, Marea Delta Pro: US$1.99 per month or US$14.99 per year.

Uses public data from Argentina's Instituto Nacional del Agua (INA). It does not replace nautical charts or Coast Guard notices.
